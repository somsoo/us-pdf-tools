Frequently Asked Questions

Is it safe to merge and process confidential PDF documents here?

Yes. All PDF operations run 100% locally in your web browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files never leave your device and are never uploaded to any cloud server.

What PDF operations are supported in this toolbox?

You can merge multiple PDF documents into one file, extract specific pages or page ranges, rotate pages clockwise by 90, 180, or 270 degrees, and convert JPG or PNG image files into a multi-page PDF.

Is there any file size limit, page limit, or watermark?

There are no artificial file size limits, page restrictions, or watermarks added. Processing speed and capacity depend entirely on your device memory.
